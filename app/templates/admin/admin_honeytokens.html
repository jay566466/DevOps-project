ℹ️ How Honeytokens Work

A hidden honeypot field is embedded invisibly in the login and register forms. Real users never see or fill it. Bots that auto-fill all form fields trigger the honeypot — the attempt is silently logged here and the request is redirected without revealing detection. No CAPTCHA is shown to the bot.
